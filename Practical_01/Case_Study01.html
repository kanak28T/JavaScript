About Symbiosis Institute of Technology

Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Nagpur is one of the leading engineering colleges in Nagpur, nurtured by the visionary leadership of Symbiosis International (Deemed University), Pune.

The institute follows a Problem-Based Learning approach, encouraging students to solve real-world challenges through hands-on projects, hackathons and industry-aligned coursework, supported by state-of-the-art labs and a fully residential campus.

Admissions open for industry-ready B.Tech programs with a strong focus on engineering and research.
